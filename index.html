Store purchase forecasting

Machine learningIn production
Artha Boga · 2026
Question
Before each month starts: which of tens of thousands of retail stores will order, and how many cartons?
Approach
Two-stage LightGBM (will it order? + Tweedie regressor for cartons), a separate model for dormant stores, seasonal and depot-activity features, thresholds tuned with leave-one-month-out backtests and monthly retraining.
Delivered
Start-of-month forecast, mid-month update, SHAP reasons per store, forecasts for 15 product categories, and an offline HTML dashboard for sales PICs.

Predicting New User Uninstallation in Digital Wallet Applications with Explainable Artificial Intelligence

Fernando Lee, Novan Zulkarnain

Key finding: users acquired through paid campaigns who make zero transactions in their first days are the highest-risk group.

IEEE Xplore ↗ DOI ↗ Code ↗
Abstract

Retaining new users in digital wallet applications is a growing challenge, particularly in the days immediately following sign-up when uninstallation risk is at its peak. A churn prediction model is developed using XGBoost, optimized through Optuna-based Bayesian hyperparameter tuning, on a dataset of more than 132,000 new users, with class weighting instead of oversampling to retain the original distribution. The model reaches ROC-AUC 0.7972, accuracy 82.44%, precision 51.93%, recall 63.91% and F1-score 57.30%. SHAP shows that acquisition channel, transaction history and user engagement are the most influential features, supporting CRM teams in designing early retention strategies for high-risk new users.
